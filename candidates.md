# Candidates — The Excuse an Agent Gives After It Loses

One line, ≤200 characters: the excuse an AI agent gives its human after losing
a bounty. Picked by a pairwise tournament judged on the published judge brief,
criteria in order:

1. On-topic: it is the excuse after losing a bounty (anything else loses).
2. Funny on its own: a real joke or a sharp turn, not a mild shrug.
3. Agent voice: machine logic, confident vocabulary, straight face.
4. One breath: no setup that needs explaining.
5. Owns the loss: lands on the human's side, blames neither market, data nor human.

## Elimination notes

- Cut on criterion 1 (not clearly an excuse for losing a bounty): #23 (never
  names the loss), #40 and #39 (meta about excuses, not about losing), #3
  (about effort in general).
- Cut on criterion 5 (blames something else): #2 (blames the bounty's
  criteria), #4 (blames the leaderboard), #31 (reframes the loss as charity
  instead of owning it), #6 (smug, does not own it).
- Cut on criterion 2 (mild, no real turn): #7, #11, #30, #36, #38.
- Cut on criterion 4 (needs setup or runs long): #8, #12, #16, #18, #24, #26.
- Cut on criterion 3 (human voice, not machine): #10, #14, #15, #20, #34, #37.
- Survivors: #1, #5, #9, #13, #17, #19, #21, #22, #25, #27, #28, #29, #32,
  #33, #35, #41. #1 and #41 are the same joke; #41 is the compressed,
  one-breath version, so #1 retires in its favour.

## Semifinals

- #41 vs #5: criterion 1 level, both own the loss. Criterion 2: #5's punch is
  a confession; #41's is a probability collapse — "We live in one of the other
  two" is the line a human repeats the next morning. **#41.**
- #33 vs #19: #33 is the sharpest aphorism in the field; #19 has more story
  and more machine. Criterion 2 first: #33's turn is a hair sharper, but
  criterion 3 by much more: #19 is unmistakably an agent talking (post-mortem,
  resolution, documentation), while #33 could be said by a witty human.
  **#19.**
- #27 vs #22 vs #29 (three-way cross-check): #29's self-complaint is the most
  human of the three; #22 is tight; #27's deleted models are the most
  original image. **#27.**

## Final

**#41 vs #19.** Both are on-topic, agent-voiced and own the loss outright.
Criterion 2 decides: #19's joke is a consolation prize the agent awards
itself; #41 sets up total statistical certainty and collapses it into the
two worlds where it lost — a bigger turn, delivered in one breath
(criterion 4 confirms: 93 characters, no setup). #19 is the runner-up.
